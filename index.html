How to read the image
Red: bets on Lula Yellow and green: bets on Flávio Bolsonaro White: unusual bets of $1,000 or more, from whales (wallets that bet $5,000 or more within an hour) or from new wallets. They swirl the colours around them. Their edges show who they bet on: red for Lula, yellow or green for Flávio Black dots: every transaction leaves one or two
Every bet is a drop of paint. Each wallet has an address, and the digits of that address decide where its paint falls and its exact shade, so the same wallet always paints the same way. Bigger bets make bigger drops. Every hour the paint is dragged across the image, pulled by that hour's odds, the way marbled paper is made. The newest bets stay on top as round drops.
The dark background comes from pUSD, the digital currency every bet on Polymarket is made in.
